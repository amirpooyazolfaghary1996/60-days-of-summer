# BoardWork 60 — the written plan

A 60-day programme for a push-up board, a yoga mat, a sturdy chair and (optionally) a sturdy table. Built for a 30-year-old, 188 cm, 70 kg trainee training for lean muscle gain.

**Board channels:** blue = chest · red = shoulder · green = triceps · yellow = back and laterals

## Structure

| Phase | Days | Focus | Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Foundation | 1–14 | Groove the patterns, build joint tolerance, learn the board. | Stop 3-4 reps short of failure. |
| 2 — Build | 15–32 | Add volume. This is where most of the size comes from. | Stop 2-3 reps short of failure. |
| 3 — Intensify | 33–49 | Harder leverages and slower tempos at slightly lower volume. | Stop 1-2 reps short of failure. |
| 4 — Peak & Test | 50–60 | Deload, then re-test. Days 50-56 are deliberately easy. | Leave 4+ reps in reserve until test day. |

The week repeats on a seven-day cycle:

| Day of cycle | Session |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Push A** — Chest priority |
| 2 | **Legs A** — Squat pattern |
| 3 | **Pull & Core** — Back, rear delts, midsection |
| 4 | **Mobility** — Active recovery, no hard sets |
| 5 | **Push B** — Shoulder & triceps priority |
| 6 | **Legs B & Core** — Hinge, unilateral, midsection |
| 7 | **Rest** — Full day off |

Days 50–56 are a deliberate deload at roughly 60% volume. Day 60 is a test day: one all-out set of each benchmark, compared against the numbers you recorded on day 1.

## Benchmarks

Record these before day 1 and again on day 60. One all-out set each, full rest between.

- **Standard Push-Up** — max reps
- **Pike Push-Up** — max reps
- **Diamond Push-Up** — max reps
- **Bulgarian Split Squat** — max reps
- **Inverted Row Under a Table** — max reps
- **Forearm Plank** — max hold in seconds
- **Hollow Body Hold** — max hold in seconds

## Notes

**Eating.** At 188 cm and 70 kg the training only pays off if the food is there. Roughly 110–150 g of protein a day and a few hundred calories above maintenance. If the scale has not moved by day 20, add food rather than sets.

**Sleep.** Seven to nine hours does more for recovery than any change to the programme.

**Anchors.** Nordic curl negatives, inverted rows under a table and handstand holds all depend on something holding your weight. Test it before you trust it. Every one of them has a listed substitute if you have no safe anchor.

**Pain.** Burning in a muscle is the point. Sharp or pinching in a joint is not — stop, switch to the listed regression, and see someone if it persists.
